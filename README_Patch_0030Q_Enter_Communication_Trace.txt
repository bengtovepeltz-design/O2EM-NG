0030Q - ENTER communication trace
Diagnostic only. Existing 0030P instruction trace is retained.
COMM BEGIN starts 300 execution slices at ENTER (about 6 seconds at PAL rate).
COMM records accepted latch writes and first reads of each generation.
SUMMARY counters are cumulative; pairs mean 8048->NSC / NSC->8048.
Repeated reads of an unchanged generation are counted, not printed.
At most 2000 detail events; summaries continue until COMM END.
Capture BEGIN through END. Reset cancels the window; another ENTER restarts it.
No CPU timing, latch selection or keyboard behavior changed.
